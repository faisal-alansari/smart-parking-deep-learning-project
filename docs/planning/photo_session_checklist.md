# Photo Session Checklist (night car park)

Goal: about **60 photos** of pairs of parking bays, all from the same angle. About 1 hour.

## Before you start

- [ ] Phone charged; plenty of free storage.
- [ ] Camera grid on (Settings → Camera → Grid).
- [ ] 1× lens, no zoom, flash off, Live Photo off.
- [ ] Hold the phone in **landscape** (sideways).

## For every photo (same way every time)

- [ ] Two bays side by side in the photo.
- [ ] Stand **centred on the line between the two bays**: left bay in the left half, right bay in the right half.
- [ ] Phone at **chest height**.
- [ ] About **4–5 steps behind** the front edge of the bays.
- [ ] Front edge of the bays in the **lower third** of the screen, with a little ground in front showing.
- [ ] No people in the photo (retake if someone walks in).
- [ ] Only **one photo per pair of bays**, then move to another pair.

## What to capture (tick as you go)

### Bay states: about 15 photos each

- [ ] Both bays **empty**: ___ / 15
- [ ] Only **left** occupied: ___ / 15
- [ ] Only **right** occupied: ___ / 15
- [ ] **Both** occupied: ___ / 15

### Hard cases: try to get each at least 3 times

- [ ] Empty bay with **cars visible behind it** (next row): ___ / 3
- [ ] Empty bay with a **strong shadow or light patch** on the ground: ___ / 3
- [ ] Car **parked badly**, crossing the line: ___ / 3
- [ ] Car parked **nose-in** (front plate facing you): ___ / 3
- [ ] Car **reversed in** (rear plate facing you): ___ / 3
- [ ] Plate with a **bright plate lamp or street-light glare**: ___ / 3
- [ ] **Dark-coloured** car: ___ / 3

## You are done when

- [ ] At least **60 photos** in total.
- [ ] At least **40 empty bays** in total (count each empty bay, not each photo).
- [ ] At least **40 different cars** with the plate facing you.
- [ ] Each bay state has at least 10 photos.

If empty bays are hard to find, keep going until you have 40; they matter most.

## Skip

- Spots so dark that you can't tell if the bay is empty.
- The same cars photographed again.
- Changing your height, distance or angle.

## After the session

- [ ] Copy all photos to `data/raw/local_night_1/`. No labelling needed.
- [ ] Tell Claude roughly how many photos you took, and anything unusual.
- [ ] Do **not** share the photos anywhere: they show strangers' plates.
